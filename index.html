Contact & booking options
How do I request an appointment?

Message Jurong West or Clementi on WhatsApp, or call the same branch number. Tell the team your preferred timing and reason for visiting. Wait for the clinic to confirm your appointment.

Which clinic offers Sunday appointments?

Clementi publishes clinical hours on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Jurong West is closed on Sundays. Contact Clementi to check availability before visiting.

Can I ask about CHAS or MediSave?

Yes. The clinic can explain which subsidies or claims may apply to your treatment. Eligibility, claim limits and your final payment depend on your circumstances and procedure. Ask for an itemised treatment estimate.

Read the CHAS information

Can I come in if I feel nervous?

Let the clinic team know when arranging your visit. You can discuss your concerns, ask what an assessment involves and speak with your dentist about the proposed care.
