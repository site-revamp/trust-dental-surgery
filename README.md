# Trust Dental Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Trust Dental Surgery is a Singapore neighbourhood dental practice founded in 2019, providing family, preventive, restorative, cosmetic and surgical care in Jurong West and Clementi. Its website helps patients explore treatments, choose a clinic and make enquiries.

The redesigned homepage clarifies branch contact options, provides direct treatment links and distinguishes Trust Dental’s two clinics from Vera Dental, its separately branded sister clinic in Tanjong Pagar.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Branch-Specific WhatsApp Actions

Clearly labelled WhatsApp buttons identify each branch, including two persistent mobile buttons. Previously, the main WhatsApp action defaulted to Jurong West without naming the branch, potentially misdirecting Clementi enquiries. Patients can now select the intended clinic before starting a conversation.

### 2. Direct Treatment Pathways

Each of the six treatment pathways links directly to its relevant page. Previously, several cards opened the general services overview, requiring visitors to search again. The revised links take patients directly to their selected treatment information.

### 3. Consistent Clinic Counts and Branding

The homepage clearly presents two Trust Dental clinics and identifies Vera Dental separately as the sister clinic. This resolves conflicting references to “two locations” and “three clinics” while preserving Vera Dental’s separate identity.

## Design Improvements

- **Contact clarity:** Branch labels distinguish WhatsApp destinations before patients make contact.
- **Navigation consistency:** Treatment pathways link to their corresponding pages.
- **Information hierarchy:** Trust Dental clinics and the Vera Dental sister clinic are clearly distinguished.
- **Mobile experience:** Two persistent WhatsApp buttons keep branch-specific enquiry options accessible.
